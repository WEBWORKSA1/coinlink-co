How we verify links

1 · Source of truth

Curated entries are taken from the organization's own documentation and long-standing public presence. Coin entries come from each project's public profile data and refresh automatically.

2 · Blocklist screening

Every domain is cross-checked against open phishing blocklists. Shared hosting platforms (github.io, pages.dev, vercel.app…) are never treated as “verified” on their own.

3 · Community reports

Projects and readers can report changed or wrong links. Reports are reviewed before changes go live. Listing is not an endorsement or investment recommendation.
