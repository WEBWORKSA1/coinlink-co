What we check

⛔

Known phishing lists

Open blocklists from ScamSniffer and MetaMask, updated automatically. Parent domains are checked too, so evil.sub.example can't hide.

✅

Verified directory

Exact and subdomain matches against verified official domains for exchanges, wallets, coins and apps.

🔤

Typosquats & look-alikes

One-letter swaps (metamsk), character tricks (0→o, rn→m), punycode alphabets and brand names on the wrong domain.

🪤

Bait words

“claim”, “airdrop”, “sync”, “validate”, “restore”, “support” and dozens more drainer favourites.

🏗️

Throwaway hosting

Free subdomains on pages.dev, vercel.app, netlify.app, web.app and similar, plus abused extensions like .xyz and .top.

🎭

URL disguises

The “@” trick, raw IP addresses, deep subdomain chains and brand-first hosts like coinbase.com.secure-login.help.

🚩 Report a scam link

Found a fake site, drainer, or impersonation account? Report it — verified reports can win the monthly Scam-Spotter bounty.

Already clicked a bad link?

  1. Stop signing. Close the tab. Don't approve any more wallet prompts.
  2. Revoke approvals with revoke.cash for each network you used.
  3. Move funds to a brand-new wallet with a new seed phrase if you typed or exposed your seed.
  4. Secure accounts: change exchange passwords, reset 2FA, check API keys.
  5. Report it to Chainabuse and your national fraud centre.

Link checker FAQ

Is my link sent to a server?

No. The analysis runs in your browser. It downloads small shards of the public blocklist and our verified list from this website. Your recent checks are saved only in your own browser.

What does the risk score mean?

0–19 means no red flags (or a verified match), 20–49 means caution, 50–79 means strong scam signals, and 80–100 means very likely or confirmed phishing. It's a heuristic, not a guarantee.

How fresh is the blocklist?

Our workflow rebuilds it from the public ScamSniffer and MetaMask lists every week. ScamSniffer's open list is published with a 7-day delay, so brand-new scams may not appear yet — the look-alike checks help cover that gap.

A legitimate site was flagged. What now?

Heuristics can misfire, especially for new or small sites that use a big brand's name. If you run the site, submit it for verification.
