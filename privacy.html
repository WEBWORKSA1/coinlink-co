Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information CoinLink.co (“we”, “us”) collects when you use https://coinlink.co (the “Site”), how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information you give us

When you submit a form (newsletter, Starter Kit, contact, listing, advertising, donation pledge, contest, job application or scam report) we receive the fields you fill in — typically your name, email address and message. We use it only to respond to you, deliver what you requested, administer contests and, if you opted in, send the CoinLink Brief. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-relay service (FormSubmit), which processes the data on our behalf.

2. Information stored only in your browser

Your watchlist, portfolio holdings, price alerts, recent link checks, theme and currency preferences are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to us. You can delete them by clearing site data in your browser.

3. Link checker

Links you paste into the phishing checker are analyzed in your browser. The page downloads public blocklist files from this Site; the link itself is not transmitted to us unless you choose to submit it through the report form.

4. Third-party services that load in your browser

  • Google AdSense serves ads on the Site. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Market and network data providers (CoinGecko, CoinPaprika, CoinLore, Alternative.me, mempool.space, public Ethereum RPC): your browser requests data directly from them, which exposes your IP address to them under their privacy policies.
  • Google Fonts for typography and YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode, loaded only after you press play) for videos.

5. Cookies and consent

We show a consent banner on your first visit. Choosing “Reject non-essential” asks Google to serve non-personalized ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may also see Google's certified consent message. See our cookie policy.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

We process form data to respond to your request (contract/pre-contract steps) and, for newsletters, based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Advertising cookies rely on consent where required.

7. Retention

We keep correspondence and form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were provided for, and newsletter data until you unsubscribe.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your personal information, or object to certain processing. Send requests through our contact form using the topic “Privacy request”. We do not sell personal information.

9. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 16 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

10. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS and stores no passwords or financial data. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
