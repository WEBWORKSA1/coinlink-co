How to read this table

Market cap

Price × circulating supply. It tells you how the market values the coins that exist today, not how much money has flowed in. Read market cap vs FDV.

Volume (24h)

Total value traded across tracked exchanges in the last day. Very low volume relative to market cap can mean thin liquidity and large price swings.
