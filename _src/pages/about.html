About us

The right link, every time

CoinLink.co exists because the most expensive mistake in crypto is usually not a bad trade — it's a bad click. Clone websites, fake support accounts and “claim” pages drain wallets every day. We built a place to find the real link and check the suspicious one.

What we do

  • Verified directory — official domains for exchanges, wallets, explorers, DeFi apps, networks and the top coins.
  • Phishing link checker — screens URLs against open blocklists and common impersonation tricks, right in your browser.
  • Live market data & tools — prices, sentiment, network fees and practical calculators.
  • Original guides — plain-English education with safety built in.

What we don't do

  • We don't hold customer funds, run an exchange, issue a token or ask for wallet access. Anyone claiming otherwise is impersonating us.
  • We don't give personalized investment advice or price predictions.
  • We don't sell verification. Paid placements are always labeled.

How the site is funded

CoinLink.co is supported by advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly labeled sponsorships, optional paid services for projects, and voluntary reader support. Our editorial policy explains how we keep commercial relationships separate from content.

Data & credits

Market data: CoinGecko, CoinPaprika and CoinLore. Sentiment: Alternative.me Fear & Greed Index. Network data: mempool.space and public Ethereum RPC endpoints. Phishing data: ScamSniffer's open scam-database (GPL-3.0) and MetaMask's eth-phishing-detect. Headlines and videos link back to their original publishers and creators.
