Trust

Editorial policy & methodology

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Our standards

  • Accuracy first. Guides are based on primary sources — protocol documentation, whitepapers, official announcements and government resources — and are reviewed before publishing and when facts change.
  • No hype, no predictions. We explain how things work and what can go wrong. We don't publish price targets or “next 100x” lists.
  • Safety built in. Every guide includes the security angle relevant to its topic.
  • Plain English. Jargon is explained or linked to the glossary.

How we verify official links

  1. Curated entries (exchanges, wallets, explorers, apps) are checked against the organization's own documentation, long-standing public presence and multiple independent references.
  2. Coin entries are synced automatically from each project's public profile on a major data aggregator and refreshed on a rolling schedule.
  3. All domains are screened against public phishing blocklists. Shared hosting platforms are never verified on their own.
  4. Projects can request changes with proof of domain control; community reports are reviewed before any change.

Commercial independence

Advertising (including Google AdSense) and sponsorships fund the site. Sponsored content is always labeled. Advertisers and sponsors cannot buy verification, change risk scores or edit our guides.

Corrections

Spotted an error? Tell us via the contact form (topic: Correction). We fix factual errors promptly and update the “Updated” date on the page.
