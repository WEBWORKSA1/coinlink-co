Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. Entry is free. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances.
  • Eligibility. Open to individuals 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live) in jurisdictions where such contests are lawful. Void where prohibited. Employees and contractors of CoinLink.co and their households are not eligible.
  • Entry period. Each contest runs for the calendar month (UTC). Prediction entries close 7 days before month-end.
  • Prizes. Prize types and values are announced on this page before each month starts and depend on sponsor and reader funding. Prizes may be paid in fiat, gift cards or a stablecoin at the winner's choice where possible. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes.
  • Judging. Scam-Spotter: impact and verification of reports. Quiz draw: random draw among qualifying scores. Prediction: closest to the CoinGecko BTC/USD price at month-end; earliest entry wins ties. Creator: clarity, accuracy and reach. Decisions are final.
  • Winners are notified by email within 7 days after the contest ends and must reply within 14 days or an alternate may be selected.
  • One entry per person per contest (Scam-Spotter: unlimited reports). Automated or fraudulent entries are disqualified.
  • Privacy. Entry data is used only to administer the contest and contact winners. See our privacy policy.
  • We may modify or cancel a contest if it cannot run as planned for reasons beyond our control.
