How to choose an exchange safely

  • Regulation where you live. Check your country's financial regulator register; many exchanges are licensed only in specific regions.
  • Proof of reserves and track record. Prefer exchanges that publish reserve attestations and have operated for years without unresolved incidents.
  • Security features. Hardware-key 2FA, withdrawal address allowlists and anti-phishing codes in emails.
  • Fees and spreads. “Zero fee” often means a wider spread. Compare the total cost with our fee break-even calculator.
  • Withdraw what you don't trade. Exchanges are custodians. Long-term holdings belong in a wallet you control — see our wallet security guide.
