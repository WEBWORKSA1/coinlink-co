How to use sentiment data (and how not to)

Sentiment indexes summarize momentum, volatility, social chatter and dominance into one number. Extreme fear has historically coincided with sharp sell-offs and extreme greed with euphoric rallies, but neither is a timing signal on its own. Use it as context alongside your own plan — for example, a dollar-cost averaging schedule that removes the need to guess.

Fees matter more than most people think. When Bitcoin's next-block fee spikes, small transfers can become uneconomic; when Ethereum gas is low, it can be a good moment to consolidate or revoke old token approvals.
