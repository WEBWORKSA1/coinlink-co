Frequently asked questions

What is CoinLink.co?

CoinLink.co is an independent crypto information site focused on one problem: getting you to the right website. We publish verified official links, a free phishing link checker, live market data, calculators and educational guides. We don't hold funds, sell tokens or give investment advice.

How does the link checker work?

It compares the domain you paste against open-source phishing blocklists (ScamSniffer and MetaMask's eth-phishing-detect, refreshed automatically), our directory of verified domains, and heuristics such as typosquatting, look-alike characters, punycode, risky extensions, free-hosting subdomains and bait words like “claim” or “sync”. The check runs in your browser.

Can a link be dangerous even if the checker finds nothing?

Yes. New scam sites appear every hour and may not be on any list yet. A “no red flags” result means we found nothing suspicious, not that the site is verified. Only domains marked “Verified official domain” match our directory.

Where do your prices come from?

Live prices load in your browser from CoinGecko, with automatic fallbacks to CoinPaprika and CoinLore, plus a cached snapshot refreshed every few hours. Prices may be delayed and shouldn't be used for trading decisions.

Is CoinLink.co free?

Yes. The site is funded by advertising, clearly labeled sponsorships and voluntary support from readers. See how to support us.
