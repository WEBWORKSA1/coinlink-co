The small units of crypto

One bitcoin is 100,000,000 satoshis (sats), named after Bitcoin's creator. Fees on Bitcoin are quoted in sats per virtual byte. One ether is 1,000,000,000 gwei and 1018 wei; Ethereum gas prices are quoted in gwei.

Thinking in sats is handy for small amounts: “10,000 sats” is easier to read than 0.0001 BTC.

FAQ

Why do wallets show different decimals?

Display settings differ, but the underlying amounts are always whole numbers of the smallest unit.
