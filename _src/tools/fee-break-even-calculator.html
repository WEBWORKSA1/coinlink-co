The hidden cost of “easy” buys

Instant-buy buttons in many apps combine a visible fee with a spread built into the price. On a $500 purchase, 1.49% each way plus a 1% spread and a withdrawal fee means you start roughly 4–5% behind. Advanced or “pro” trading screens on the same exchange are often far cheaper.

FAQ

What's a spread?

The gap between the buy and sell price. Brokers can earn from it even when they advertise “zero fees”.
