Risk first, position second

Professional traders decide how much they're willing to lose before they decide how much to buy. If you risk 1% of a $10,000 account ($100) and your stop is 8% below entry, your position can be $1,250 — losing 8% of $1,250 is $100.

Stops can slip in fast markets and gaps, so real losses can exceed the plan. Leverage magnifies both outcomes and adds liquidation risk.

FAQ

What risk % is typical?

Many traders use 0.5–2% per trade so that a losing streak doesn't wipe out the account.
