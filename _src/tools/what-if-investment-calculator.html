Hindsight is free — use it wisely

This calculator looks up the coin's price on the date you choose and values the same number of coins at today's price. It's a great way to understand volatility: try the same coin from a market top and from a market bottom and compare.

Remember survivorship bias: the coins in today's top 100 are the winners. Thousands of coins from past cycles went to zero, and a “what if” on them would show a loss of nearly everything.

FAQ

Why does it say no price was found?

Free data sources cover roughly the last 12 months for most coins (longer for majors via our stored monthly history). Enter the price manually if you know it.

Does it include fees or taxes?

No — it's a simple price comparison.
