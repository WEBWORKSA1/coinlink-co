What the result means

We deduct the buy fee from your investment, convert the remainder into coins at your buy price, value them at your sell price and deduct the sell fee. ROI is profit divided by what you put in. Break-even sell price is where you'd walk away with exactly your original investment after both fees.

Fees compound: 0.5% in and 0.5% out means the price must rise about 1% before you're even. On low-liquidity coins the spread can cost more than the fee, so check the order book before trading.

What fee should I enter?
