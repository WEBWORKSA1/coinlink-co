How crypto gains are usually taxed

In many countries, selling, swapping or spending crypto is a taxable disposal. Your gain is the proceeds minus your cost basis (what you paid, including fees). Some countries tax assets held longer than a threshold — often 12 months — at a lower rate; others tax a portion of the gain or treat frequent trading as income.

Because rules vary widely, this estimator lets you enter your own rates. Keep complete records of every transaction, and consider tax software or a professional for anything beyond simple cases.

FAQ

Is swapping one coin for another taxable?

In many jurisdictions, yes — it counts as selling the first coin.
