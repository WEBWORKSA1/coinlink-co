APY vs APR

APY already includes compounding; APR doesn't. We treat your input as an APY and convert it into a per-period rate for the compounding frequency you pick, so changing frequency doesn't inflate the result.

Staking rewards are paid in tokens. If the token's price falls 50%, a 5% yield won't save you — that's why the calculator also models price change. Consider lock-up periods, slashing, validator fees and smart-contract risk with liquid staking.

FAQ

Are staking rewards taxable?

In many countries rewards are taxed as income when received. Check local rules.

What is slashing?

A penalty that destroys part of a validator's stake for misbehaviour or downtime, which can affect delegators.
