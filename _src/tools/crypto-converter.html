How the converter works

The converter multiplies your amount by the coin's latest market price, pulled live from CoinGecko (with automatic fallbacks). Choose a currency to see the local price; tick “reverse” to see how many coins a given amount of money buys.

The figure is a reference rate — the volume-weighted average across many exchanges. The price you actually get depends on the exchange, its spread and fees. Use the fee break-even calculator to see what those costs do to your trade.

FAQ

Why is the converter price different from my exchange?

Each exchange has its own order book. Aggregated prices smooth those differences out, and exchanges also add spreads and fees.

How often do prices update?

Every time you change an input, using data cached for up to a minute to respect the provider's rate limits.
