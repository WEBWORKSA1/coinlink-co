Why market cap, not price, is what matters

A coin at $0.50 isn't “cheaper” than one at $500 — what matters is the total valuation. Price × circulating supply = market cap, so the implied price is the target market cap divided by the coin's circulating supply.

Use this as a sanity check on bold predictions: if a forecast implies a market cap larger than the world's biggest companies, the forecast is probably wrong. Also compare fully diluted value (FDV) — future token unlocks can expand supply and dilute holders. See market cap vs FDV.

FAQ

Why is circulating supply used?

Market cap is conventionally calculated on circulating supply. If many tokens are still locked, FDV gives a more conservative picture.
