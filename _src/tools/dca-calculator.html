What is dollar-cost averaging?

DCA means investing the same amount on a fixed schedule regardless of price. When prices are low your fixed amount buys more coins; when high, fewer. It removes the pressure of timing the market and smooths your average cost — but it doesn't guarantee profit, and in a steadily rising market a lump sum usually ends ahead.

FAQ

Why can't I choose every coin?

The backtest needs years of monthly prices. We store long histories for 20 major coins; for others, free data covers only about a year.

Does it include fees?

No. Recurring buys on many apps charge 1–2%, which matters over years. Look for low-fee recurring buy options.
