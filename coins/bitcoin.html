How to stay safe with Bitcoin

Scammers clone popular project sites and promote them through search ads, fake support accounts and “claim” pages. Before you connect a wallet or sign a transaction, compare the address bar with the official links above, or paste the URL into the CoinLink phishing checker, which screens it against more than 400,000 known phishing domains.
